More Than A JPEG

The Machine is designed to become part of a future washing mechanism for eligible wallet dust and unwanted balances.

THE PROBLEM

Crypto wallets can become messy very quickly.

Your wallet collects everything.

Small token balances, dust coins, forgotten assets and random leftovers can sit inside your wallet for months or years. Washing Machine is built around the idea of turning that mess into something useful.

UTILITY

Holder Utility
